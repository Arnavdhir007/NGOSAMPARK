💡 What Is Financial Literacy?

Financial literacy means understanding how money works and knowing how to make responsible financial decisions.

It helps us understand concepts like saving, budgeting, spending, banking, digital payments, investing and financial safety.

The earlier we learn these skills, the more confidently we can manage money throughout our lives.

💰 Money Basics

Let's start with the fundamentals.

💵

Income

Money received through sources such as salary, business, allowances or other legitimate sources.

🛒

Expenses

Money spent on things we need or want.

🐷

Savings

Money kept aside instead of being immediately spent.

📈

Investments

Money put into financial assets or opportunities with the expectation of potential future returns.

🎯 Needs vs Wants

One of the simplest financial lessons can make a huge difference.

🏠

NEEDS

Things that are important for everyday life and well-being.

  • 🍎 Food
  • 🏠 Basic shelter
  • 📚 Education
  • 👕 Essential clothing
  • 🏥 Healthcare
VS
🎮

WANTS

Things that make life enjoyable but aren't always essential.

  • 🎮 Extra games
  • 👟 Extra shoes
  • 🍔 Eating out
  • 📱 New gadgets
  • 🎬 Entertainment

📱 Digital Payments & Financial Safety

Technology makes money easier to use — but we also need to use it safely.

📱

Digital Payments

Learn how digital payment systems work and use them responsibly.

🔐

Protect Your PIN

Never share sensitive banking credentials with others.

🔑

Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords for important accounts.

🚨

Watch For Scams

Be cautious of suspicious links, messages and unexpected requests.

🚨 Think Before You Click

Financial safety is just as important as financial knowledge.

🎁

"You Won A Prize!"

Unexpected prize messages asking for money or personal information should be treated with caution.

📲

Suspicious Links

Avoid clicking unknown links or entering sensitive information on suspicious websites.

👤

Fake Support

Be careful when someone unexpectedly claims to represent a bank or service.

🔒

Never Share Secrets

Never casually share passwords, PINs, OTPs or other sensitive authentication information.

📈 Understanding Investing

📚

⚖️

Understand Risk

⏳

Think Long Term

Financial goals often require patience and planning.

🧠

Avoid Blind Decisions

Don't invest simply because someone says something is guaranteed.

❓ Financial Literacy FAQ

Let's clear up some common questions.

Financial literacy is the knowledge and understanding needed to make informed financial decisions.

Learning basic financial concepts early can help students develop responsible money habits.

A budget is a plan for how money will be earned, saved and spent.

Saving generally means setting money aside for future use, while investing involves putting money into assets or opportunities with potential future returns and risk.

Be cautious with unexpected messages, suspicious links and requests for sensitive financial information.
